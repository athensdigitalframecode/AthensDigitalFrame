Self-initiated concept · fictional company

E-shop · Streetwear drop

ENUMA ELISH · Last Train

A drop for the same fictional label: an Athens metro platform meets Tokyo's last train. The train pulls into the station as you scroll. The bag works; the products and prices are samples.

View live →Request a free demo
athensdigitalframe.vercel.app/last-train

01 — Brief

The brief we set.

The brief: a fashion e-shop that feels like an art-directed label rather than a template, drawn from the Athens metro and Tokyo street culture. There is no client.

It was built as a Shopify theme with eight sample products. This is a static version of its home page: the bag stays in your browser and there is no checkout.

  • TypeSelf-initiated concept project
  • ClientNone; fictional company
  • ScopeDesign and build
  • Liveathensdigitalframe.vercel.app/last-train

02 — Direction

Design direction.

Tunnel black, tile grey, iron green and sodium yellow: the colours of the platform. Station photographs, and copy in Greek, English and Japanese.

The navigation talks like a metro: the "next stop" changes with each section, a departure board counts down and the footer is a line map.

  • #ECE9E1
  • #F2B705
  • #24342A
  • Commissionerheadlines and text

03 — Components

What it contains.

  1. 01

    Train hero

    The train arrives frame by frame as you scroll, while a departure board counts down.

  2. 02

    The rail

    Eight pieces on a rail that moves sideways; each one turns when you hover over it.

  3. 03

    Campaign

    Platform photographs with parallax and copy in three languages.

  4. 04

    Collection and bag

    A grid with category filters, quick add by size and a slide-out bag.

04 — Behaviour

Phone and motion.

  • ResponsiveOn phones the train loads half the frames and the rail becomes a strip you swipe with your finger.
  • MotionIf you have asked your device for reduced motion, the parallax stops and the train follows the scroll without easing.
  • SamplesThe brand, products, prices, fabrics and shipping times are samples. The bag stays only in your browser; the checkout and the newsletter form send nothing.
