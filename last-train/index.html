CAMPAIGN FW26 · ΚΑΜΠΑΝΙΑ · キャンペーンSHOT ON THE PLATFORM, AFTER THE LAST TRAIN

Προσοχήστο κενό.Mind the gap.

Athens gave us the platform. Tokyo gave us the patience.

Green iron columns, a ceiling the colour of old cream, one sodium tube running the length of the wall, and a yellow line nobody crosses. Enuma Elish was drawn here, between the last announcement and the last train.

From Tokyo we took the quiet: the bow before the doors, the station that apologises for your wait, the discipline of a cut that says nothing it doesn't need to.
