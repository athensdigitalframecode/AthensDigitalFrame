SHIPPING

Orders ship from Athens within 1–2 business days.

  • Greece: 1–3 business days, €4. Free over €120.
  • EU: 2–5 business days, €6. Free over €120.
  • UK, US, Japan: 4–8 business days, from €15. Duties may apply.

You get a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves us.

RETURNS

You have 14 days from delivery to return any item, for any reason. It must be unworn, unwashed and have its tags attached.

Start a return from the link in your shipping email. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return reaching us. Exchanges for another size are free within the EU.

SIZE GUIDE

Everything is cut oversized. Measurements are of the garment laid flat, in centimetres. Each product page also lists the model's height and the size they wear.

FAQ

WHEN IS DROP 02?+

Drop 02 brings accessories. Join the newsletter at the bottom of the page and you’ll hear first.

WILL SOLD-OUT SIZES COME BACK?+

Each drop is made once. Some core pieces return in later drops in new colours.

HOW DO I PAY?+

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal through a secure checkout. Prices include VAT.

CONTACT

Questions about an order, sizing or collabs. We answer within one business day.

PRIVACY

This demo stores nothing on a server. Your bag is kept only in your own browser (local storage) so it survives a page reload; clearing site data removes it. The forms send nothing. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

A live store would publish a full privacy policy and a cookie banner here.
