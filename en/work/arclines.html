Self-initiated concept · fictional company

Logistics · Asia-Pacific

Arclines Freight

A corporate website for a fictional logistics company. The statistics, testimonials and news are samples and do not correspond to real customers.

View live →Request a free demo
athensdigitalframe.vercel.app/arclines

01 — Brief

The brief we set.

Arclines Freight is a made-up Asia-Pacific logistics brand: air and ocean freight, customs brokerage, warehousing and project cargo. There is no client.

We set ourselves a corporate brief: a lot of information, several audiences, and a brand that has to look dependable before it looks clever.

  • TypeSelf-initiated concept project
  • ClientNone; fictional company
  • ScopeDesign and build
  • Liveathensdigitalframe.vercel.app/arclines

02 — Direction

Design direction.

Near-black and a single electric blue, with ice and paper tones for the light sections.

A technical display face for headlines, a geometric sans for reading. The globe with its routes is drawn in code on a canvas; it is not a video.

  • #1A1DE6
  • #BFE3FF
  • #E9EDF6
  • Tomorrowheadlines

03 — Components

What it contains.

  1. 01

    Five services

    Air Freight, Ocean Freight, Customs Brokerage, Warehousing and 3PL, Project Cargo.

  2. 02

    Scroll-driven scenes

    A ship, a truck and a container stacker move as you scroll.

  3. 03

    Proof points, as samples

    Statistics, testimonials, news and FAQ: all samples, there to show the structure.

  4. 04

    Quote form

    Demo only: it shows the flow and sends nothing.

04 — Behaviour

Phone and motion.

  • ResponsiveOn phones the navigation becomes a drawer, and the layout was checked in portrait and landscape.
  • MotionThe scenes are driven by scroll position. If you have asked your device for reduced motion, the page respects it.
  • SamplesThe company, statistics, testimonials, client names and news are placeholders.
