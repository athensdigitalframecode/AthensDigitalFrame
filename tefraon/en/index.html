Nothing remainsunchanged.

Nature is our beginning. Fire is our medium. Transformation is our craft.

At TEFRAON, Greek ingredients, elemental techniques and contemporary imagination come together through the transformative power of flame.

01Origin

Before the flame, there is nature.

Lamb from the northern hills. Aubergines heavy with summer. Wild oregano, thyme, coarse sea salt and green olive oil. Everything begins raw, honest and unaltered.

02Ignition

Fire changes everything.

  1. Raw

    Ingredients arrive whole. We read them before we touch them.

  2. Flame

    Oak and olive wood burn down to an even, breathing heat.

  3. Craft

    Turning, resting, listening. Char where it gives depth, never where it hides.

  4. Dish

    What reaches the table carries the memory of the fire.

03Transformation

Instinct meets precision.

Chef Alexandros Vardis cooks where the room can see him, at a hearth of basalt and iron. His kitchen follows the seasons of Greek land and sea, and the slow logic of embers.

“The fire decides the pace. I only listen.”
