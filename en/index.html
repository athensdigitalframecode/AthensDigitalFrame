07 — FAQ

Before you message us.

Websites

What does the website include?

Mobile-first design, fast loading, a contact form, Google Maps and local SEO basics, for up to 3 pages.

Will it look good on mobile?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and we check its mobile speed before it goes live.

Will I rank first on Google?

We set up the local SEO basics, but no one can promise a position in Google results.

E-commerce

What does the e-shop include?

Setup (200–300€ one-time) includes the domain, SSL, a base theme, payment setup (Viva Wallet or Stripe) and up to 5 sample products. The monthly subscription (30–50€/month) covers cloud hosting, weekly automatic updates, daily backups and access to the video library.

How do I add products later?

You add them yourself, using the video tutorials.

Which payments and shipping are supported?

Payments with Viva Wallet or Stripe, cash on delivery and flat-rate shipping.

Can I list on Skroutz/BestPrice or use BOX NOW?

Yes, as paid add-ons: €150 for Skroutz/BestPrice and €120 for BOX NOW.

Pricing

How much does a website cost?

A website costs €300 for up to 3 pages. Larger sites are quoted by complexity. Our prices are not subject to VAT.

Do I pay before I see anything?

No. The free demo comes before any payment.

Is there a referral program?

Yes. For each business that signs up with us, you get €50 cash or 2 months of free maintenance. Your friend gets 10% off.

Hosting & maintenance

What happens after the site is live? What does maintenance cover?

Maintenance is €20 per month and covers regular updates, backups, security and small changes to text and photos.

What if I skip maintenance?

Your site stays online and you only pay the €50 per year for hosting and the domain.

Process

How fast does it go live?

In 3–5 days.

What do you need from me?

Your services, photos, logo and colours, collected with a short questionnaire or a 15-minute call. We study your business and your area as part of the process.

Ownership & support

Who owns the domain of my website?

The domain is yours and is registered in your name. The €50 per year covers hosting and the domain.

How do I contact you?

By DM on Instagram, on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.
