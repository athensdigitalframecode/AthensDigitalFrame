About について

From chaos, form.

Enuma Elish is a fictional Tokyo streetwear label: dark, editorial, slightly mysterious. The name comes from the Babylonian creation epic, where order is pulled out of chaos.

Each drop is a small run, cut and sewn in Japan, and never restocked. The store is built around that rhythm: a rail of the newest pieces, a drop list instead of a sale calendar, and product pages that answer sizing, shipping and duties before anyone has to ask.

The project

Concept e-commerce store designed and built by Athens Digital Frame. Products and prices are fictional.

  • PlatformCustom Shopify theme (Liquid, Online Store 2.0); this page is a static demo of it
  • Signature momentsAnimated garment rail, scroll-driven "chaos to order" sequence
  • CommerceFilterable shop, variant-aware product pages, cart drawer with free-shipping progress
  • QualityWorks without JavaScript, keyboard and screen-reader friendly, respects reduced motion
