E-shop · Streetwear

ENUMA ELISH

An e-shop for a fictional streetwear label between Tokyo and Athens. The bag works; the checkout and the products are samples.

View live →Request a free demo
athensdigitalframe.vercel.app/enuma

01 — Brief

The brief we set.

ENUMA ELISH is a brand we made up: oversized clothes inspired by Tokyo backstreets and the walls of Exarchia. There is no client.

The brief: an e-shop with the identity of a lookbook that still sells like a store. Reliable size selection, a clear bag, upfront shipping and returns.

  • TypeSelf-initiated concept project
  • ClientNone; fictional company
  • ScopeDesign and build
  • Liveathensdigitalframe.vercel.app/enuma

02 — Direction

Design direction.

Washed black, concrete grey and dirty white, with one deep red used only as a fill. Black-and-white photos with film grain.

Huge condensed headlines, monospace text, and Japanese and Greek as a third voice. Square corners everywhere, no gradients.

  • #111111
  • #858585
  • #EAE7E0
  • #A51F25
  • Big Shoulders Displayheadlines

03 — Components

What it contains.

  1. 01

    Lookbook home page

    Hero, a two-city brand story, a campaign and an asymmetric product grid with filters.

  2. 02

    Catalogue and search

    Collections with filters and sorting, plus instant product search.

  3. 03

    Product page

    Colour and size with live stock, a size guide per garment type, the model's height and a sticky add-to-bag on phones.

  4. 04

    Bag

    A slide-in bag with quantities and a free-shipping meter. The checkout is a demo.

  5. 05

    Help and contact

    Shipping, 14-day returns, FAQ and a contact form (demo only).

04 — Behaviour

Phone and motion.

  • ResponsiveOn phones the product photos become swipeable and the add-to-bag button stays fixed at the bottom.
  • MotionScrolling tickers with a pause button and one reveal on the title. If you have asked your device for reduced motion, it all stops.
  • SamplesThe brand, products, prices, stock and policies are samples. The bag stays only in your browser; the checkout and forms send nothing.
