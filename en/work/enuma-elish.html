Self-initiated concept · fictional company

E-commerce · Streetwear, Tokyo

Enuma Elish

An e-shop for a fictional Tokyo streetwear brand. The products, prices and stock are samples; nothing is sold.

View live →Request a free demo
athensdigitalframe.vercel.app/enuma-elish

01 — Brief

The brief we set.

Enuma Elish is a brand we made up: dark, editorial streetwear from Harajuku and Shibuya, in small runs that are never restocked. There is no client.

We set ourselves an e-commerce brief: a store with character that stays quick to buy from, and product pages that answer sizing, shipping and duties before the customer has to ask.

  • TypeSelf-initiated concept project
  • ClientNone; fictional company
  • ScopeDesign and build
  • Liveathensdigitalframe.vercel.app/enuma-elish

02 — Direction

Design direction.

Near-black, off-white and a single vermilion accent. Thin lines, no rounded corners, light grain, and small details in Japanese.

The garments are drawn in code (SVG) instead of photographs, and they change colour when you pick a colourway. The theme is a custom Shopify theme; the portfolio shows a static demo of it.

  • #0B0B0C
  • #EDEBE6
  • #E5383B
  • #8D8B86
  • Unboundedheadlines
  • Inter Tighttext
  • Noto Sans JPJapanese details

03 — Components

What it contains.

  1. 01

    The rail and "chaos to order"

    A rail of garments that sway and turn, and a scroll scene where fabric pieces become a hoodie.

  2. 02

    Shop with filters

    Eight sample products, filters for type, size and availability, and sorting, all kept in the URL.

  3. 03

    Product page

    Colourways, sizes that sell out per colourway, a size guide in cm and inches, and a drop-list sign-up when a piece sells out.

  4. 04

    Cart and checkout

    A slide-out cart with a free-shipping bar. Checkout is a demonstration and takes no payment.

04 — Behaviour

Phone and motion.

  • ResponsiveOn phones the menu becomes a drawer, the rail scrolls sideways, and the first tap turns a garment while the second opens it.
  • MotionThe "chaos to order" scene is driven by scroll. If you have asked your device for reduced motion, the page respects it, and it also works without JavaScript.
  • SamplesThe brand, products, prices and stock are invented. The cart stays in your browser only; checkout and the sign-up form send nothing.
