- Static, separate from the generated site; their JS/CSS are NOT hashed, so cached copies hide changes (Ctrl+Shift+R).
- Content is fictional and labelled as a concept. Never invent real clients or claims.

- Previews on the home page and case studies use `assets/<name>-d.webp` / `-m.webp`; retake them if the demo's look changes.
