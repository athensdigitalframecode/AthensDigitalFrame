Olivane Estate

A website for a fictional property developer: the home builds itself in front of you as you scroll.

View live →Request a free demo
athensdigitalframe.vercel.app/olivane

01 — Brief

The brief we set.

Olivane Estate is a company we made up: a developer building homes in Limassol, Cyprus. There is no client; we set the brief ourselves.

The question was how to show a home that is still being built. The answer became the page itself: as you scroll, the house goes from blueprint to structure to the finished home at dusk.

  • TypeSelf-initiated concept project
  • ClientNone; fictional company
  • ScopeDesign and build
  • Liveathensdigitalframe.vercel.app/olivane

02 — Direction

Design direction.

Dark, cinematic and quiet. A night-blue background lets the warm evening photography carry the page.

Headlines are set in a high-contrast serif and text in a tight sans. The accent colours are peach, coral and teal.

  • #070A12
  • #FFBD80
  • #FF6F61
  • #5EE6D0
  • Instrument Serifheadlines
  • Inter Tighttext and interface

03 — Components

What it contains.

  1. 01

    Scroll-to-build story

    Three stages in one scroll: blueprint, structure, finished home.

  2. 02

    Property listings

    Six sample properties as cards, with filters for type, area, bedrooms and price.

  3. 03

    Site-visit and brochure forms

    Demo forms: they show the flow and send nothing.

04 — Behaviour

Phone and motion.

  • ResponsiveImages are served as AVIF with a WebP fallback, in separate sizes for phones.
  • MotionThe build story is driven by scroll position. If you have asked your device for reduced motion, the page respects it.
  • SamplesThe company, its history, its figures and its listings are invented. The forms send nothing.
