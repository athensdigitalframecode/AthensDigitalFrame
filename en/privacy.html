Privacy policy

In short: this site sets no cookies, has no accounts and does not store what you type.

Last updated

11 November 2026

Who we are

Athens Digital Frame is a small digital studio in Athens, Greece. For anything about your data, write to athens.digitalframe@gmail.com.

The contact form

The form does not send anything to us or to a server. When you press the button, it opens WhatsApp or your email app with a prepared message. We receive it only if you press send there. From that point the message is handled by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own terms.

Messages you send us

If you contact us by Instagram, WhatsApp, phone or email, we use what you tell us only to reply and to prepare your demo or project. We do not sell it or pass it to anyone for advertising.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they open. According to Vercel, it uses no cookies, the data is anonymous and not tied to an individual or an IP address, and a visitor session is discarded after 24 hours. Vercel's policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it processes technical request data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies, which is why there is no cookie banner.

The concept demos

The two concept sites (Olivane Estate, Arclines Freight) load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when you open them. Their forms are demonstrations and send nothing.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to the email above. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).
